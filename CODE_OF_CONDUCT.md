# Community standards

Paracoat Dispatch Studio welcomes constructive contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give actionable feedback about code and ideas.
- Keep discussion focused on improving the project and its users' workflows.
- Protect privacy: use synthetic examples and never post private customer, shipment, route, or credential data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, or sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for conduct that violates these standards. Report concerns privately through the security reporting guidance in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or contact the repository owner through GitHub.
